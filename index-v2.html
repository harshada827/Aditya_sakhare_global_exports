Why Global Buyers Partner With Us

Quality Food, A Brighter Tomorrow

Aditya Sakhare Global Exports combines direct farm-belt proximity in Maharashtra with rigorous export documentation and transparent trade execution.

1. Direct Plant & Farm-Gate Sourcing

Located in the agricultural heartland of Maharashtra (Latur) with direct tie-ups across Gujarat's Mahuva dehydration cluster, eliminating middleman markups.

2. Batch-by-Batch Lab Verification

Every consignment undergoes moisture, microbiology, pesticide residue, and aflatoxin testing—with SGS or third-party pre-shipment inspection available upon request.

3. Shipment-Specific Export Documentation

Complete, error-free export documentation including Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, FSSAI Compliance, and port-required shipment clearances.

4. Fast Port Connectivity

Rapid dispatch via Nhava Sheva (JNPT) and Mundra ports ensures 3–7 day ocean transit to Jebel Ali (UAE), Dammam/Jeddah (KSA), Hamad (Qatar), and Sohar (Oman).

Transparent International Trade

Incoterms & Buyer Payment Structures

Flexible, bank-compliant international settlement options designed for smooth customs clearance and long-term B2B partnerships.

Supported Incoterms

EXW (Ex-Works India), FOB (Nhava Sheva / Mundra Port), and CIF / CFR (Jebel Ali, Dammam, Jeddah, Doha, Muscat, Salalah, and global ports).
